## Why this license

improve it. The license keeps that part free while leaving the
commercialization rights with the copyright holder. Same model as
[n8n](https://n8n.io/), [CockroachDB](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/),
and other "fair-code" projects.

It is **not** an OSI-approved open source license. It is a

policy that only permits OSI-approved licenses, ClipEngine is not for
